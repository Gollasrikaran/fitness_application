# Cervical Spondylosis

## Overview
Cervical spondylosis is a prevalent condition affecting individuals over 40, characterized by neck and shoulder pain, often in people over 40. It is the medical term for neck problems caused by age-related "wear and tear" to bones and discs in the neck.

## Symptoms
The condition presents with:
- Neck and shoulder discomfort or stiffness that fluctuates
- Headaches originating at the base of the neck
- Many people experience no symptoms at all, as this represents a normal part of aging

## Self-Care Measures
Recommended approaches include:
- Performing gentle neck exercises
- Correcting postural habits
- Using over-the-counter pain relief such as paracetamol and ibuprofen

Pharmacists can suggest stronger pain medication if needed.

## When to Seek Medical Attention

**Non-urgent (see a GP):** Consult a GP if pain persists beyond several weeks or worsens with movement.

**Urgent (call NHS 111):** Seek help for severe sudden pain, arm/leg weakness, tingling sensations, or shoulder blade pain.

**Emergency (call 999):** Call for walking difficulties, loss of bladder or bowel control, sudden loss of coordination, facial drooping, arm weakness, or speech problems.

## Clinical Treatment
Medical professionals examine the neck, test reflexes, and may order imaging. Treatment involves exercises, maintaining activity levels, and potentially muscle relaxants for chronic pain. Surgery is reserved for nerve compression or spinal cord involvement.

## Causes
Age-related wear and tear of spinal bones and discs cause the condition, with increased risk following previous neck injuries or in people with a family history.
