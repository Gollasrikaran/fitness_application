# Polymyalgia rheumatica

Polymyalgia rheumatica (PMR) is a condition that causes pain, stiffness and inflammation in the muscles around the shoulders, neck and hips. It's an age-related condition, mainly affecting people over 65, and rarely occurs in people under 50. It's more common in women.

## Symptoms

- muscle stiffness, especially in the morning, that usually lasts longer than 45 minutes
- extreme tiredness
- loss of appetite
- weight loss
- depression

## Causes

The exact cause of PMR is not known, but a combination of genetic and environmental factors is thought to be responsible.

## Treatment

The main treatment is a steroid medicine called prednisolone. You'll usually be started on a moderate dose, which is then gradually reduced over time. Most people need a course of steroid treatment that lasts for 12 months to 2 years to prevent their symptoms returning.

## When to see a GP

See a GP if you have pain and stiffness that lasts for more than a week.

## Temporal arteritis warning

Up to 1 in 5 people with PMR also develop a related condition called temporal arteritis (giant cell arteritis), which needs to be treated urgently to prevent serious complications such as vision loss or stroke. Warning signs to look out for include:
- a sudden, severe headache
- pain in the jaw or tongue when eating or talking
- blurred or double vision, or loss of vision

Contact NHS 111 or seek urgent medical help immediately if you develop any of these symptoms.
