## What is (are) Herniated Disk?

The spine consists of 26 vertebrae with soft disks between them that provide cushioning. As people age, these disks degenerate and lose their cushioning ability, potentially causing pain.

A herniated disk occurs when the disk ruptures, allowing the jelly-like center of the disk to leak, irritating the nearby nerves. This can result in sciatica or back pain.

Diagnosis involves a physical exam and sometimes imaging tests. Treatment options include rest, pain management with anti-inflammatory medicines, physical therapy, and sometimes surgery. Most people recover with appropriate treatment.
