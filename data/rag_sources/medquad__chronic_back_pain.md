## What is Back Pain?

Back pain is one of the most prevalent medical conditions, affecting 8 out of 10 people at some point during their lives. The condition manifests as either acute (sudden onset lasting days to weeks) or chronic (persisting beyond three months). Symptoms can range from dull, constant aches to sharp, sudden pain and may result from trauma, sports injuries, or everyday activities.

## Treatments for Back Pain

Management approaches focus on restoring function and preventing recurrence. Treatment options include:

- Over-the-counter pain relievers and anti-inflammatory medications
- Hot or cold compresses to reduce pain and inflammation
- Limited bed rest (maximum 1-2 days; prolonged rest can worsen symptoms)
- Exercise to strengthen back and abdominal muscles
- Physical therapy and medications
- In severe cases, surgical intervention

The goal is to restore proper function and strength to the back, and prevent recurrence of the injury.

## Outlook for Back Pain

Most patients recover favorably. However, individuals should contact a doctor if there is not a noticeable reduction in pain and inflammation after 72 hours of self-care. Recurring pain from poor body mechanics is often preventable through proper ergonomics, correct lifting techniques, and appropriate exercise. Early intervention and proper preventive measures significantly improve long-term outcomes.
