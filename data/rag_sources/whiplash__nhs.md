# Whiplash

Whiplash is a neck injury caused by sudden movement of the head. It usually gets better within 2 to 3 months. It's most often caused by road traffic accidents or falls, and symptoms can take several hours to appear after the injury.

## Symptoms

- neck pain and stiffness, with limited movement of the head
- headaches
- pain and muscle spasms in the shoulders and arms

## Self-care

**Do:**
- take over-the-counter painkillers, such as paracetamol or ibuprofen
- try to carry on with your normal activities as this can help you recover more quickly

**Don't:**
- do not wear a neck brace or collar - these are not recommended
- do not keep your neck still for long periods

## When to get help urgently

Contact NHS 111 or get an urgent GP appointment if you have:
- severe pain that isn't helped by painkillers
- tingling or pins and needles
- difficulty walking or standing normally
- an electric shock feeling in your neck and back that spreads to your arms or legs
- weakness in your arms or legs

These symptoms can be a sign of nerve damage.

## Treatment

A GP may prescribe stronger painkillers, such as codeine. If symptoms haven't improved after a few weeks, you may be referred for physiotherapy, to see a pain specialist, or for psychological support. Physiotherapy is available both on the NHS and privately.
