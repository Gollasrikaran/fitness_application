AGENT CONTEXT FEATURES
======================

What the agent gets to know about a patient, read from the live clinic
database (read-only), keyed by the clinic's patient id.

Code: backend/app/clinic.py -> patient_context()
Shown in the app: the "What the agent sees" panel, next to the chat.


WHO USES THE AGENT
------------------
Patient mode  - a patient chats with the agent about themselves.
Doctor mode   - a doctor picks one of their patients and asks the agent
                about that patient.
Only adults (18+) are listed. Deleted, test (is_demo) and merged patient
records are never shown.


FEATURES WE ARE TAKING
----------------------

1. About the patient
   - Age, sex, preferred language, date registered.
     (patient.age_years, sex, preferred_language, created_at)

2. Body measurements
   - Latest weight and height, and the BMI worked out from them, with the
     date measured. (visit.vitals_json: weight_kg, height_cm)

3. Health conditions
   a. Recorded by a doctor, with severity and status.
      (patient_condition + long_term_condition)
   b. Health-history questionnaire answers marked "present", with any notes:
      hypertension, diabetes, thyroid, heart disease, asthma,
      high cholesterol, tuberculosis. (patient.health_history_json)
   c. The patient's long-term conditions list. (patient.long_term_conditions)
   d. Diagnoses from the last 10 visits, provisional and confirmed.
      (prescription.provisional_diagnosis, confirmed_diagnosis)

4. Vital flags
   - Abnormal and critical vital readings from the last 5 visits: blood
     pressure, pulse, SpO2, temperature, sugar, haemoglobin. Comes from the
     clinic's own versioned flag rules. Readings flagged IMPLAUSIBLE (likely
     typing errors) are left out. (visit_vital_flag)

5. Recent vitals
   - BP, pulse, SpO2, temperature, sugar, haemoglobin, weight from the last
     10 visits, with dates. (visit.vitals_json)

6. Current medicines
   - Medicines on the latest prescription: name, dose, frequency, duration.
     (prescriptionitem)
   - The doctor's latest instructions to the patient, and the follow-up date
     they set. (prescription.patient_instructions, follow_up_at)

7. Ongoing care
   - Visit count, first and last visit dates.
   - Recent complaints, with how long they've lasted and which doctor saw
     them. (visit.chief_complaint, complaint_duration)
   - Follow-ups: how many are pending (and the next due date), how many
     were missed. (followup_schedule)
   - Open disease episodes. (patient_disease_journey: OPEN, ACTIVE_FOLLOWUP)
   - Open care tasks, e.g. patient outreach. (care_task: OPEN, CLAIMED)

8. Alerts
   - Visits marked as emergencies. (prescription.is_emergency)
   - Referrals to a specialist. (prescription.is_referral_recommended,
     referral_specialist_name, referral_treatment_name)

9. Other history
   - Surgeries, hospitalisations, drug allergies, smoking, alcohol, with
     notes, when marked "present". (patient.health_history_json)


NOT TAKEN YET (later)
---------------------
- The rule-based clinical snapshot from dataanalysis/ (flags graded
  high / medium / info). Needs to be called from dataanalysis.
- The AI clinical review from dataanalysis/. Only exists after a clinician
  opens the patient in that dashboard.
- Lab results from uploaded reports (OCR). Left out on purpose.


NEVER TAKEN
-----------
- Patient name, phone number, address, government ID, family links.
- Uploaded documents and photos (visible to doctors and coordinators only).
- Consultation video and session data.
- Staff passwords and IDs. (Doctor names are shown, as staff.)
